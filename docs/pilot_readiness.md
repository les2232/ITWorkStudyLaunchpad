## Maintenance and portfolio boundary

Must finish before maintenance-only status: organizational decisions, actual provider/account lifecycle checks, actual-host authorization and recovery, browser/accessibility acceptance and owner sign-off for the invited release. Appropriate maintenance afterward is dependency/security patches, revocation/provisioning, backup/restore drills, safe content corrections, monitoring and fixes to observed failures. New dashboards, gamification, broad analytics, messaging integrations, an LLM assistant and a major redesign remain outside this release.

Known bounded limits: admin/mentor roles are read-only in the web app; readiness remains a human judgment; unfinished lab interaction state is session-local; some POST refreshes create another recorded attempt. The audit table records local event/target IDs, not comprehensive read access or human operator attribution; the organization must decide whether restricted operational records suffice. Backup scheduling/transport/encryption and retention execution are operator responsibilities, not implemented hosted services.

A sanitized portfolio case study can describe the onboarding problem, focused navigation, verified isolation/scoring/persistence and the remaining evidence gaps, subject to permission to discuss employer-owned work. It cannot yet claim proven reduced mentoring time, a less busy experience for intended users or a successful real-user launch. Obtain authorized task outcomes/feedback first; publish only approved sanitized diagrams, synthetic screenshots and aggregate results, never code, identity mappings, institution-specific procedures, secrets or learner records without explicit authorization.

**Scope can be frozen now, but the app is not finished enough to move into maintenance or accept real learners.** The remaining work is the defined identity/hosting decisions and release verification, not product expansion.
